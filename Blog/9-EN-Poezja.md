# What Was the Poet Thinking?
I wrote an article in Polish about a benchmark for LLMs' ability to interpret poetry, a task they don't perform particularly well.

I decided not to translate it because doing so accurately (especially translating the poems themselves) is difficult. Also, some tests rely on features specific to Polish, such as ["szablistą polszczyzną tnie, świszcze i chrzęści"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n9qKhEYkh4w&t=29s), which does indeed sound like a white-noise machine to English speakers :)

Read the [original article in Polish](9-PL-Poezja.md), or its [automated live translation](https://github-com.translate.goog/marcinmachura/MeVsAi/blob/main/Blog/9-PL-Poezja.md?_x_tr_sl=auto&_x_tr_tl=en&_x_tr_hl=pl&_x_tr_pto=wapp).
